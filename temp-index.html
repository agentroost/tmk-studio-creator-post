Tmk Studio Creator Post

Tmk Studio is a creator studio that sells digital kits for creators. Creator Post is its companion tool for creators who publish their own videos on TikTok.

How it works:

  1. You connect your own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. You choose one of your own videos and send it to your own TikTok inbox through the TikTok Content Posting API (Upload mode).
  3. You open the TikTok app on your phone, review the video in your inbox, and press Post yourself. The app never publishes anything for you.

The app only accesses basic profile information (to show which account is connected) and the video you explicitly choose to send. It does not sell personal data, and it does not post content on your behalf.
