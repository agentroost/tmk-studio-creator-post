Privacy Policy

Tmk Studio Creator Post — Last updated: November 19, 2026. Operated by Tmk Studio. Contact: t0mek21yt1@gmail.com.

1. What this app does

Creator Post helps a creator send their own video to their own TikTok inbox using the TikTok Content Posting API (Upload mode). The creator then opens the TikTok app on their phone, reviews the video, and presses Post themselves. The app never publishes content on the user's behalf.

2. Data we access

Only with your consent through TikTok Login Kit and the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information (TikTok Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): your TikTok display name, username, profile picture, and open ID — used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload (Content Posting API, scope video.upload): the video file you explicitly choose to send, transmitted to TikTok so it appears in your own inbox. The video is not used for any other purpose.
  • Authorization tokens: the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues for your connection, used only to act on your instructions. Tokens are revoked when you disconnect the app.

We do not access your TikTok password, your followers, your messages, or any video you do not choose to send.

3. How data is used and shared

Data is used only to provide the service described above. We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with TikTok, as needed to connect your account and deliver a video to your inbox at your request, and as required by law.

4. Retention

Profile information and tokens are kept only while your account is connected. Videos are transmitted to TikTok on your instruction and are not retained by Tmk Studio after delivery. When you disconnect, tokens are revoked and connection data is deleted.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect Creator Post at any time from your TikTok account settings (authorized apps) or by contacting us. You can also ask us to delete data associated with your connection by emailing t0mek21yt1@gmail.com.

6. Children

The service is intended for creators who meet TikTok's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy.
